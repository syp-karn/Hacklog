# Kerberos Delegation Overview

Kerberos delegation allows a service to impersonate a user to access other resources or services on behalf of that user.

## Types of Delegation

### 1. Unconstrained Delegation
- The service receives a copy of the user's TGT (Ticket Granting Ticket) in its TGS response.
- The service can store this TGT in LSASS memory and reuse it to authenticate to any service in the domain as the user.

### 2. Constrained Delegation (S4U2Proxy / S4U2Self)
- Restricted to specific target services (SPNs).
- Service uses `S4U2Self` to get a service ticket for itself on behalf of any user.
- Service uses `S4U2Proxy` to exchange that ticket for a ticket to target services specified in `msDS-AllowedToDelegateTo`.

### 3. Resource-Based Constrained Delegation (RBCD)
- Configured on the **target resource** (`msDS-AllowedToActOnBehalfOfOtherIdentity`) rather than the delegated service account.
- Allows principals with permission to write to this attribute on a computer object to configure delegation rights.
